# Job Alignment Rubric
*How the `score-backlog` skill scores a job against the candidate in `.alignment-info.md`. Every job scoring helper receives both files.*

---

## Scoring Rubric

Score a posting **4–5** if it includes most of the following:
- Senior or Lead Product Designer title (or Staff/Principal equivalent)
- Small autonomous team structure with real ownership
- Complex platform, B2C or B2B2C product
- Values prototyping, systems thinking, or design-dev collaboration
- Agile environment that adapts process to context
- Regulated, mission-driven, or technically complex domain
- Explicitly values or requires AI fluency in design workflow

Score a posting **2–3** if:
- Role skews heavily visual/brand without systems or UX depth
- Large design org with heavy process overhead and low ownership
- Research-heavy role where synthesis is the primary function
- Junior-leaning scope despite senior title
- Pure enterprise B2B with no product design culture

Score a posting **1** if:
- Management/Director role with no IC design work
- Pure graphic/marketing design
- Role requires skills clearly outside background (e.g. motion design lead, hardware UX)
- Significant seniority mismatch (either too junior or too senior with no IC component)
